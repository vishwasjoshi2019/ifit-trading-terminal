# IFIT V0.6.8 — REGIME ENGINE

Adds market-environment classification on top of V0.6.7.

Regimes:
- TRENDING BULL
- TRENDING BEAR
- RANGE / MEAN REVERSION
- VOLATILITY EXPANSION
- HIGH-RISK TRANSITION
- NO EDGE
- BULLISH/BEARISH BIAS / MIXED

Outputs:
- Regime confidence
- Directional strength
- Flow alignment
- Range score
- Transition risk
- Regime drivers
- Cross-module diagnostics

The classifier separates market direction from volatility/risk and is not a
probability-of-profit forecast or automatic trade signal.
