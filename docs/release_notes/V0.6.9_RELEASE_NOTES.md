# IFIT V0.6.9 — SIGNAL ENGINE

Adds the decision-intelligence layer above V0.6.7/V0.6.8.

Outputs:
- IFIT Signal: BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL
- Direction Score 0–100
- Conviction score and label
- Risk state
- Market regime
- Support / resistance
- Expected-move zone
- Conservative invalidation boundary
- Signal driver attribution
- Transparent methodology

Direction is led by Market State and Flow, with PCR as a low-weight positioning
input. Conviction accounts for cross-module agreement and risk/model penalties.
This is a research classification, not an automatic trading instruction or
probability-of-profit forecast.
