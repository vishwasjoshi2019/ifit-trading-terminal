# IFIT V0.6.4 — LIVE HESTON MODEL INTELLIGENCE

Builds on V0.6.3 Live Volatility Surface and the existing Heston calibration/model
engines.

Features:
- Interactive Heston calibration against the active option IV smile
- v0, kappa, theta, sigma_v, rho parameters
- Price RMSE and IV RMSE
- Market IV vs calibrated Heston IV comparison
- Calibrated Heston IV surface
- Explainable model-dislocation ranking
- Dislocation score and regime
- Calibration-quality diagnostics
- 30-second Streamlit cache for interactive recalculation

The module is research/model intelligence. It does not automatically execute
trades or claim arbitrage from model residuals.
