# IFIT V0.7.7 — Market Microstructure / Execution Intelligence

Adds a cross-contract microstructure layer on top of V0.7.6 Liquidity & Execution.

## New intelligence
- Microstructure Score (0–100)
- Execution Friction score
- Microstructure regime
- Median and P90 spread diagnostics
- Quote quality and source tracking
- Five-level depth and top-depth diagnostics
- Queue concentration proxy
- ATM CE-vs-PE microstructure pressure diagnostic
- Highest-quality contract map
- Highest-friction contract map
- ATM execution impact ladder for 1/5/10/20 lots
- Estimated spread cost and slippage in rupees
- Top-5 OI concentration diagnostic
- Transparent methodology and simulation-proxy labelling

## Data policy
Provider bid/ask/depth fields are preferred when supplied by an authorized feed. In simulation mode, IFIT derives deterministic quote/depth proxies from LTP/OI and labels them clearly. OI is not treated as real order-book depth.
