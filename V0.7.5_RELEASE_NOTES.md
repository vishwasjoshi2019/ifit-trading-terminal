# IFIT V0.7.5 — Portfolio Risk Engine

## Added
- Portfolio-level normalized risk score and regime.
- Directional / Delta risk, convexity risk, volatility risk and theta/carry risk.
- Third-order risk using Speed, Color, Ultima and Veta.
- Position concentration risk and position-level risk attribution.
- Multi-dimensional stress cube across spot, IV and time shocks.
- Worst/best modeled scenario and modeled loss-rate diagnostics.
- Tail-bias classification: DOWNSIDE / UPSIDE / TWO-SIDED.
- Strongest risk-driver ranking and explainable methodology.
- Risk alerts for extreme/high/concentrated/tail-risk portfolios.

## Important
These are normalized research diagnostics, not statistical VaR, margin requirements,
capital requirements, forecasts, or automatic trading instructions.
